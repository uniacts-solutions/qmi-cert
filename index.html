ISO Certification Process with QMI

We follow a streamlined and transparent approach so that your team always knows what comes next

1

Initial Discussion & Scope

We begin with a consultation call to understand your activities, locations, existing systems and the ISO standard(s) you require. Based on this, we propose the most suitable certification path.

2

Documentation & System Setup

Our team shares the required policies, procedures, formats and checklists, and guides you on how to adapt them to your organization. We focus on practical documentation that your team can actually use.

3

Internal Review / Gap Assessment

A gap assessment or internal audit is conducted (onsite or remotely) to check where you stand against ISO requirements. We then help you close nonconformities and strengthen implementation.

4

Stage 1 Audit

An accredited certification body reviews your documented system, scope and readiness. Any observations from this review are addressed before moving forward.

5

Stage 2 Audit

The main certification audit is carried out—verifying records, interviewing staff and reviewing operations to confirm that your management system is effectively implemented.

6

Certificate Issue & Ongoing Support

On successful completion of the audit, you receive an ISO certificate under IAS / EGAC / UAF accreditation. We continue to support you with guidance for annual surveillance audits and continual improvement.
